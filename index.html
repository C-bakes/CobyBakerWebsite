Sheet 03 / 04 Sheet metal forming

Cold pressed plate armour

Hand formed gauntlets, cold pressed from 1mm mild steel and shaped without moulds. Getting the curvature right meant working out the springback compensation first, then pressing, measuring, and adjusting before pressing again. The finished pieces are being 3D scanned now, with moulds in progress to scale production.

Made in a backyard workshop with clamps and hand tools. No moulds, no press brake, no hydraulic forming.
